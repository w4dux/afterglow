# Afterglow 2.6.0

Full-screen kinetic lyrics for Windows. Afterglow follows Spotify (or any app in Windows' media controls), finds the lyrics and puts each line on stage as it is sung. No account, no Spotify login.

## Download

- **Afterglow-Setup-2.6.0-x64.exe**: installer (per user).
- **Afterglow-2.6.0-win-x64.zip**: portable; unzip and run `Afterglow.exe`.

Not code-signed yet: Windows SmartScreen will warn on first launch. Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## New in 2.6.0

- **Zine**, a new theme for punk and DIY: a cut-and-paste fanzine where every word is a scrap in its own typeface, slapped down as it's sung.
- **The controls now hide properly while music plays.** They used to stay on screen when playback started from Spotify, or after clicking a button.
- **Tidier controls:** the top bar fits small windows, the Music & lyrics and Timing panels are easier to read, and the NEW badge is readable.

## Since 2.0.2

- **Seventeen themes**, including Halo, Monsoon, Departures, Haze, Rave, Singalong and Zine. Auto picks one from the song's genre, using Apple's public catalogue since Spotify doesn't share genres.
- **Better sync with Spotify:** Spotify's short song lengths and late pause reports are handled. Saved lyrics are kept per song.
- **Beginner-friendly start:** a one-click "Follow Spotify" welcome, plain-language controls, and a "Copy diagnostics report" button.
- **Duets and translations** when the lyrics include them, and battery-friendly frame pacing.

Lyrics come from LRCLIB (community lyrics) and the AMLL TTML database. Lyrics with only line timing are never shown as if they had word timing.
